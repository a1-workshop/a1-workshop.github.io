Gate_Circuit_lite

Validate resistor-divider math for the Environmental Monitor front-end. The agent proposes values; you run the gate; a human records Approve or Reject.

01 · Open Instructor brief

Goal: Prove the conditioned sensor voltage lands inside the ADC window without editing the checker to force a pass.

Why this gate: Agents invent numbers. The harness recomputes divider ratio, impedance, and series membership independently of the LLM.

Done when:

  • You ran the gate on a design (not the LLM grading itself)
  • You observed known-bad fail adc_window
  • You recorded Approve or Reject in the audit log
